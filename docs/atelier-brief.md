# Reference-led series: requirements and completion gates

Goal: revisit the earlier requirements and produce different full website versions resembling the two supplied references, with straighter boundaries between blocks and a slightly stricter treatment. The user permits sustained work for up to roughly nine hours; time alone is not a completion criterion.

Authoritative starting state: clean source commit `811e9e8797e6f59a9e94f1d2c991e83ac9ff5ec5`. Both new reference images were inspected on 27 October. Earlier gallery and restart series remain historical material, not the acceptance standard for this series.

The current series will contain six complete directions, all in the required order: header, hero, service, about Anastasia, process with family outcomes, pricing/conditions, genuine reviews, final request, footer/contact details.

1. Blue portrait: closest to the new reference, cafe photo and one restrained quotation; open three-column service and horizontal process.
2. Personal page: warm paper, portrait on the left and an editorial column system; stacked pricing and letter-like reviews.
3. Contemporary grid: sans-serif headings, modular blue/sage layout and practical interview examples; accessible interactive process.
4. Photo cover: broad photographic introduction with a readable light caption panel; open article sections and a pricing ledger.
5. Detailed profile: sage, an example profile as the main service result and a smaller portrait; clear comparison of service formats.
6. Questions and answers: typographic opening, structured family questions, an explanatory process and a centered request form.

All directions must differ in composition and section treatment, not just colours. Every page must remain coherent internally. All section boundaries are straight. Card/button/photo rounding is moderate and deliberate. Decorative illustrations, waves, marketing counters, fake statistics and invented claims are excluded.

Content rules: retain the approved headline; use factual, professional Russian without sentimental slogans or excessive informality. Biography: since 2022, two agencies, earlier five years working with children. Service: family-specific interviews, detailed profiles with comments, unlimited candidates and six-month free replacement for full selection only. Draft prices: selection from 25,000 roubles; consultation 5,500 roubles. Use the supplied testimonials and actual supplied photos. No invented credentials, candidate data, contacts or reviews. Telegram remains `https://t.me/anastasiadoroninaa`.

Layout rules: site shell at most 1120px; content columns narrower; visible outer margins on wide screens. At most two font families per page, consistent heading hierarchy, comfortable body text. Mobile must retain word spacing when visual breaks are hidden, clear buttons and all nine sections.

Completion requires evidence for each direction:

- rendered desktop, wide-screen and mobile review, including full-page screenshots;
- at least one critique and correction cycle, followed by recapture of changed output;
- visible distinction from the other five directions, assessed from rendered pages;
- complete content and section-order checks, intact photos, readable pricing and request form;
- menu, service selection and honest local Telegram draft behavior;
- overflow and width checks at 320, 390, 768, 1440, 1920 and 2560px;
- appropriate build/static checks and meaningful tests after major changes/before publication;
- verified GitHub source and GitHub Pages publication; preserve the private Sites audience;
- a usable gallery of all six current versions, with full screenshots and old work retained.

Status evidence and unfinished work belong in ignored `project-notes/atelier-2026-10-27/`. Do not mark the goal complete merely because templates exist or tests pass.
