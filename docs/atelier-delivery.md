## Verification

Each direction has before/after complete desktop and mobile screenshots and an independent visual critique. Corrections include the mobile photo caption, early request buttons, a distinct editorial opening, removal of the duplicated profile, visible access to all five tabs and narrow-screen text fixes. Final screenshot reviews report no remaining mandatory visual defects.

The browser matrix covers all six pages at 320, 390, 768, 1440, 1920 and 2560px. The final 36 observations show no page or text overflow; wide-screen shells are centered at 1120px. Mobile menus, Escape, navigation closure, consultation selection, local draft creation/invalidation and privacy dialogs were checked on every page. The five tabs were clicked individually and checked with End, Home and ArrowRight; all five native disclosures were closed and reopened. Gallery filters, favorite persistence, empty results and reset were exercised.

`npm run build`, `npm run check`, `npm test` and `git diff --check` passed before publication. The static check covers local assets, anchors, labels, image dimensions, modules and JavaScript syntax across the distribution. The 16 tests include section order, the approved headline and the scope of replacement/unlimited candidates to full selection only. Visual distinction is established by rendered review, rather than inferred from catalog metadata.

Published lightweight previews and complete desktop/mobile WebP captures are in `dist/atelier/`. Original PNG evidence, geometry, interaction results and critique reports are retained locally in the ignored `project-notes/atelier-2026-10-27/` directory. `scripts/prepare-atelier-previews.py` exports reviewed captures; it does not create browser screenshots.

## Prototype boundaries

Prices and timelines come from the supplied material and carry a visible note to confirm them before a working launch. Forms prepare a message locally for the visitor to send in Telegram; they do not transmit or store contact details. A production submission service and its approved personal-data documents remain a subsequent launch task.
