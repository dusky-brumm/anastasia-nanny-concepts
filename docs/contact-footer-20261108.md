## Исходный промпт

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a small elegant dry-crayon illustration for the consultation form of a personal nanny-selection website. A quiet invitation to talk: two simple comfortable armchairs angled gently toward each other, one very small round table between them, with just two little cups. No people. The entire scene is expressed by sparse, confident, visibly grainy wax-crayon and soft pastel-pencil strokes, slightly thick and broken dry lines, restrained cross-hatching. Refined adult editorial drawing, graceful simple shapes, warm and personal, plenty of open areas inside the objects, very few details. No photorealism, no smooth vector finish, no flat solid blocks, no childish doodles, no 3D, no watercolor wash. Colors: muted slate blue #658392 for one chair and contour, muted sage green #8c9f85 for the other chair, tiny warm cream/ochre accents on the cups, almost-white chalk highlights. The chairs are small with delicate legs, not big sofas. Composition: one horizontal vignette centered in the canvas, clear recognizable silhouette, all objects fully visible, transparent surrounding area, no large ground shadow, a tiny broken grounding stroke only. True transparent alpha background, no paper rectangle, no text, no lettering, no border, no watermark. Must remain beautiful and legible when displayed only 200 px wide on milk #fcfcfa background.

Refine this small website illustration. Keep exactly the two armchairs, tiny round table and two cups, slate blue and sage palette, dry-crayon grain, complete horizontal composition and genuine transparent background. Make it much airier and simpler: the chairs are drawn mostly with elegant broken dry-crayon contour strokes and only a few light hatching marks; erase most of the dense interior pigment so the transparent background shows through. Both chair backrests must be plain uninterrupted curves with NO decorative holes, NO heart cutouts, NO circles or openings. Slimmer arms, visually lighter furniture, more adult editorial sketch, fewer details. Preserve recognizable shapes. No people, no added elements, no text, no opaque paper rectangle, no shadows.
